Cancellation Policy

This Cancellation Policy explains how order cancellations are handled at AR Technology. By placing an order with us, you agree to the terms mentioned below.

1. Order Cancellation Window

Orders can be cancelled only before dispatch. Once an order has been packed or shipped, cancellation requests will not be accepted.

2. How to Request Cancellation

To request a cancellation, customers must contact AR Technology via WhatsApp or Instagram with their order details as soon as possible.

3. Cancellation Confirmation

All cancellation requests are reviewed manually. A cancellation is considered successful only after confirmation from our team.

4. Refund on Cancellation

If an order is cancelled before dispatch, a refund may be initiated as per our Refund Policy. Please refer to the Refund Policy for more details.

5. Non-Cancellable Orders

Orders that have already been dispatched, customized kits, or special bulk orders are not eligible for cancellation.

6. Payment Processing

Refunds for approved cancellations are processed within the timelines mentioned in our Refund Policy.

7. Policy Updates

AR Technology reserves the right to update this Cancellation Policy at any time. Changes will be effective once posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

For cancellation-related queries, please contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram with your order details.
