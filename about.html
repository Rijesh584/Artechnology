About AR Technology

Empowering Makers with Quality Electronic Components

Who We Are

AR Technology is a dedicated electronics components supplier committed to fueling the curiosity of the maker community. We serve as a bridge between complex engineering concepts and the hands-on reality of building projects.

Our primary focus is supporting students, hobbyists, DIY makers, and innovators who are exploring the worlds of robotics, IoT (Internet of Things), embedded systems, and academic research projects.

What We Do

We don't just sell parts; we provide the building blocks for innovation. Our catalog is carefully curated to ensure that every component—from the smallest resistor to the most advanced development board—meets our standards for quality and reliability.

  • Supply high-quality, tested electronic components.
  • Provide project-ready parts that are ready to be integrated.
  • Offer specialized guidance to help students navigate their academic projects.
  • Simplify the shopping experience via direct WhatsApp and Instagram ordering.

Why Choose AR Technology

In a market full of choices, we stand out by being a maker-first business:

  • Reliable Components: No more "dead on arrival" parts; we source with care.
  • Student-Friendly Pricing: We keep costs low to ensure learning remains affordable.
  • Fast Response: Get quick support and order updates via WhatsApp.
  • Curated Kits: Specialized combos designed for specific learning paths.
  • Honest Support: If a part isn't right for your project, we'll tell you.

Our Mission

To make electronics learning affordable, practical, and accessible to every aspiring engineer and hobbyist.

Our Vision

To become the leading catalyst for innovation, supporting the next generation of engineers who will shape the future of technology.
