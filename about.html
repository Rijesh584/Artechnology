About Us

AR Technology is an electronics components provider focused on supporting students, hobbyists, and makers. We help individuals turn ideas into working projects by providing reliable components and practical support.

Who We Are

We are a small, maker-focused electronics store dedicated to learning, experimentation, and innovation. Our goal is to make electronic components easy to access and easy to understand.

AR Technology primarily serves students, DIY enthusiasts, robotics learners, and engineering aspirants working on academic and personal projects.

What We Do

We supply a wide range of electronic components and modules required for learning, prototyping, and project development.

  • Electronic sensors and modules for projects
  • Development boards and controllers
  • Motors, drivers, and power components
  • Project kits and basic electronics tools

Why Choose Us

  • Reliable and tested components
  • Affordable pricing for students
  • Quick support through WhatsApp
  • Simple and transparent ordering process

Our Mission

Our mission is to make electronics learning practical, affordable, and accessible for students and makers at every level.

Our Vision

Our vision is to support the next generation of innovators by providing quality components and honest guidance for real-world projects.
