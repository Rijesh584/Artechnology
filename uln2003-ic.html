ULN2003 Darlington Array IC

₹52

The ULN2003 is a high-voltage, high-current Darlington transistor array commonly used to drive relays, stepper motors, solenoids, LEDs, and other inductive loads. It contains 7 Darlington pairs with built-in flyback diodes for protection.

Specifications

  • Channels: 7 Darlington transistor pairs
  • Output Current: Up to 500mA per channel
  • Output Voltage: Up to 50V
  • Input Compatible with TTL & CMOS
  • Built-in Flyback Diodes
  • Package: DIP-16

Applications

  • Stepper motor drivers (28BYJ-48)
  • Relay driving
  • Solenoid and actuator control
  • LED and lamp drivers
  • Arduino & microcontroller projects
