Shipping Policy

This Shipping Policy outlines how orders placed with AR Technology are processed, shipped, and delivered. By placing an order with us, you agree to the terms mentioned below.

1. Order Processing

All orders are processed manually after confirmation through WhatsApp or Instagram. Orders are usually packed within 1–3 working days after payment confirmation.

2. Shipping Locations

We currently ship orders across India. International shipping is not available at this time.

3. Delivery Timeline

Estimated delivery time may vary based on location, courier partner, and product availability. Typical delivery time ranges between 3–7 working days after dispatch.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are communicated during order confirmation and may vary depending on order size, weight, and delivery location.

5. Courier Partners

Orders are shipped using trusted third-party courier services. AR Technology is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners or external factors.

6. Order Tracking

Tracking details will be shared via WhatsApp once the order is dispatched, wherever applicable.

7. Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping addresses. AR Technology will not be liable for delays or losses caused due to incorrect address details.

8. Delivery Issues

In case of delayed, lost, or damaged shipments, please contact us immediately. We will assist you in coordinating with the courier service wherever possible.

9. Policy Updates

AR Technology reserves the right to update this Shipping Policy at any time. Changes will be effective once published on this page.
