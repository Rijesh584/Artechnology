L293D Motor Driver Shield

₹220

The L293D motor driver shield is an Arduino-compatible shield designed to control DC motors, stepper motors, and relays. It stacks directly onto Arduino Uno boards for easy motor control.

Specifications

  • Driver IC: L293D Quad Half-H Driver
  • Motor Voltage: 4.5V – 36V
  • Logic Voltage: 5V
  • Max Current: 600mA per channel
  • Supports up to 4 DC motors
  • Supports 2 Stepper motors

Applications

  • Arduino robotics projects
  • Motor control experiments
  • Line follower robots
  • Educational robotics kits
  • Automation systems
