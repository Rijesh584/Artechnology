What does AR Technology sell?

AR Technology specializes in electronic components for students, hobbyists, and DIY makers. Our product range includes:

  • Development Boards: Arduino, ESP32, ESP8266, Raspberry Pi
  • Sensors: Temperature, motion, ultrasonic, gas, and more
  • Communication Modules: Bluetooth (HC-05, BLE), Wi-Fi modules
  • Displays: OLED, LCD, TFT, LED matrices
  • Power Supplies: Adapters, batteries, voltage regulators
  • Accessories: Jumper wires, breadboards, resistors, capacitors

We're constantly updating our inventory to meet the needs of makers and tech enthusiasts!

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Here's our return and refund policy:

Defective Products: If you receive a defective or damaged item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos/videos. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund.
